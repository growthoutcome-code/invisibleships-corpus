## What this month contains
Three typed entries from 2 July were written at a shelter, on a bus and at a day center (IS-J01-20250702-ENTRY). On 10 July the author begins repeating statements aloud into a phone recorder and transcribing the audio with an AI tool. All 52 recordings fall between 10 and 17 July, made at an intersection where he holds a donation sign, on walks and scooter rides around Denver, and outside shelters. On 11 July he loses his shelter bed for arriving after curfew. That night's recordings are angry, and in some of them the author demands euthanization from unseen parties. His own notes say these were said in anger and that he does not accept euthanization (IS-J01-20250711-ENTRY, IS-J01-20250711-R13). Entries for 12–16 July are stubs listing recordings; the month closes with a typed entry (IS-J01-20250727-ENTRY) and a summary note (IS-J01-20250728-ENTRY).

## Recurring statements
- **Being recorded.** Statements react to the recordings: the transcripts are "word for word" and "incriminating", and he is told to stop (IS-J01-20250710-R02, IS-J01-20250711-R02).
- **The intersection.** Passing drivers are told "Do not support this person." A "battle for the intersection" is named, and statements and the author say that repeating the statements cuts this by about half (IS-J01-20250710-R03, IS-J01-20250711-R06, IS-J01-20250713-R04).
- **Euthanization and execution.** Statements urge the author to accept euthanization, describe it as ethical or a service, or threaten to execute him. Other voices object (IS-J01-20250711-R01, IS-J01-20250712-R02, IS-J01-20250715-R01).
- **"Speakerphone."** This is the author's name for statements he hears as audible sound coming from street noise. Statements link it to cell towers and satellites, and he asks whether other people hear it too (IS-J01-20250710-R06, IS-J01-20250712-R03, IS-J01-20250717-R03).
- **Breaching.** Statements describe things the author saw or felt, and he records these as possible evidence that others share his senses. He asks what this means for passwords and finances (IS-J01-20250715-R01, IS-J01-20250717-R06).
- **Automation and rotation.** Statements and the author raise the idea of AI voice agents. "Let's move on to the next person" is described as marking rotating groups (IS-J01-20250712-R01, IS-J01-20250715-R02).
- **Named organizations.** A federal security agency, a technology company, a drug-enforcement agency and foreign governments are named often. The author notes that names are sometimes appended to supportive statements (IS-J01-20250712-R03, IS-J01-20250713-R01).
- **A nuclear-strike statement.** On 17 July a statement claims a coming nuclear strike. The author treats it as character defamation and lists the homes he has lost (IS-J01-20250717-R05, IS-J01-20250717-R06).

## The author's own entries
The author's spoken "administrative notes" make up much of the recordings. He timestamps and locates himself, offers theories about the technology and asks whether bystanders hear it. He proposes a high-court test case and consent-based rules for telepathic communication, and asks for help for his daughter and ex-wife. In IS-J01-20250713-R06 he recounts a suicide attempt from before the statements began, which he stopped by seeking hospital care. Questions & Comments appear in IS-J01-20250710-R07, IS-J01-20250711-R17 and IS-J01-20250727-ENTRY. In the 28 July note he says that without financial support he cannot continue editing the document.
