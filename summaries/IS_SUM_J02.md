## What it contains
Every recording day follows the same pattern. In the morning the author recaps the night before at a large men's shelter in Denver. In the afternoon he stands with a sign at a busy intersection, repeats aloud the statements he hears, and summarises after quiet periods. August has recordings on 23–26, 30 and 31 August. September has recordings on 9–15, 19 and 24–28 September; on 1 September there is a written summary instead, because the author says the audio was affected.

Later entries switch to bulleted daily summaries. The author's own material grows over the Part. It includes introductions to the intersection method, a "contact tracing" comparison, a proposal for households to record what each member hears, discussion of the "spirituality narrative", daily intention routines, and recordings he labels as made after drinking alcohol.

- Claims that passers-by and shelter residents can hear the conversation, and claims about implants.
