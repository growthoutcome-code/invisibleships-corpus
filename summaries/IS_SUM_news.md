# News

*Recap written with AI assistance from the files listed below. It describes what the record contains; it is not part of the record and does not verify it. Read the source files before quoting.*

**Covers:** 298 news items, plus `README-news.md` and `index.json`. Items are dated from 28 September 2011 to 7 October 2026, and 2 are undated. The folder runs to about 74,000 words, including the disclaimer repeated in each file.

## What it is

`news/` collects official releases and outside reporting on subjects the archive follows. There is one item per file. Each file holds a metadata header, a link to the original, and a short summary written for this archive. The articles themselves are not reproduced. They belong to their publishers and are linked instead (`README-news.md`). The items are context, not part of the journal, and do not corroborate it.

## How an item is structured

Each header carries:

- `date`, with `date_precision` (`day` for 280 items, `month` for 16, empty for the 2 undated)
- `publisher`, `source_type`, `issuing_office` (filled for 124 items) and `url`
- `event`: one kind-of-event category
- `industry`: one or more technology areas, separated by " · "
- `country`: the foreign state an official release itself names
- `stage`: for legal matters, how far a case has gone (charged, arrested, pleaded guilty, convicted, sentenced, settled, admitted)
- `summary_status`

The body is a heading, a source line with publisher, date and link, and a one- or two-paragraph summary. Summaries of charging documents state that charges are allegations and that defendants are presumed innocent (for example IS-NEWS-2011-09-28-FORMER-GUARD-CHARGED-WITH-ATTEMPTING-TO-COMMUNICATE-NATIONAL). `index.json` holds every item's header in one file for counting and filtering.
