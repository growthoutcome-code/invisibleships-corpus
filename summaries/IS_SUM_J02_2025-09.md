## What this month contains
The daily pattern from August continues. In the morning the author recaps the night at a 300-person shelter in Denver. In the afternoon he stands at an intersection with a cardboard sign and alternates between repeating statements aloud, listening and summarising, and silence. The 1 September entry says recordings were "negatively impacted" and gives a written summary instead (IS-J02-20250901-ENTRY).

Three changes stand out. From 15 September, statements from a group claiming to speak for a foreign government appear, alongside claims about who operates the "systems" (IS-J02-20250915-R01, IS-J02-20250915-R02). From 26 September, the author reports experiencing other people's point of view while asleep (IS-J02-20250926-R01, IS-J02-20250927-R02). Small protests at the intersection on 13 and 27 September prompt statements about being sent there (IS-J02-20250913-R01, IS-J02-20250927-R05).

## Recurring statements
- **"Do not support this person (to the left of you)."** Aimed at drivers and often followed by "add them to the list." The author asks whether drivers actually responded (IS-J02-20250910-R01, IS-J02-20250912-R01, IS-J02-20250925-R04).
- **Accepting euthanization.** Statements ask why he has not accepted it. These include a statement asking if he would consider ending his own life and a whiteboard visual urging it. He refuses each time (IS-J02-20250915-R01, IS-J02-20250927-R01, IS-J02-20250927-R07).
- **Threats to family and to him.** Statements threaten his daughter and ex-wife if he keeps recording. Others claim harm to a longtime friend (IS-J02-20250909-R02, IS-J02-20250914-R01, IS-J02-20250925-R01).
- **"Word for word accuracy."** Speakers comment on whether his transcripts are accurate. The author analyses why this keeps coming up (IS-J02-20250909-R03, IS-J02-20250914-R04).
- **Who is speaking.** Statements name agencies, a contractor and foreign groups as operators, sometimes denying it later. The author says all of it must be substantiated (IS-J02-20250915-R04, IS-J02-20250928-R03, IS-J02-20250928-R04).
- **"Spirituality narrative" versus technology.** The author and respondents discuss whether the voices are presented as spirits to avoid seeking consent (IS-J02-20250927-R04).
- **Offers and lures.** Statements offer drugs, a ride in a van or money if he goes somewhere. The author calls these lures (IS-J02-20250919-R01, IS-J02-20250928-R07).
- **Attack claims.** Statements claim a terrorist attack since February, a nuclear threat and an attack overseas (IS-J02-20250919-R06, IS-J02-20250925-R05).

Typical lines: "This is absolutely the worst product launch." (IS-J02-20250909-R03); "Hold your ears shut and see if there's a difference." (IS-J02-20250927-R06).
