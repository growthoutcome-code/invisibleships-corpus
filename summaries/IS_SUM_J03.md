## What it contains
The author records in and around Denver. He sleeps in shelters and stands at a street intersection with a cardboard sign asking for donations. Most recordings follow two patterns:
- **Morning recaps** of statements heard overnight and of detailed "point of view" visuals.
- **Intersection sessions** where he repeats aloud what he says passing drivers are told.

Several recordings are mostly in his own voice: a self-care routine, research notes on how assisted dying is regulated, and safe first-contact terms (no questions in person, an envelope, a video call, an attorney). On 16 October he records that no organisation he sent the transcripts to has made contact, and that he has posted a link to all the audio.

Over the Part his own framing changes. Early October is "public awareness". Late October introduces the "override" technique. November brings a "silencing" technique and a staged "journey".

- Statements naming agencies, local police and companies, and offers of drugs, which he calls predatory.
- Long night-time visuals of violence at an "offsite location". He says each time that they must be substantiated.
- More frequent supportive or defensive voices, such as "This person needs to be treated with dignity and respect" (`IS-J03-20251026-R01`).
