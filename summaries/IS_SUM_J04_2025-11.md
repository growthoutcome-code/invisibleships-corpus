## What this month contains
Recording took place on six days (11, 13, 14, 19, 21 and 29 November) in Denver. Most days open with a recording about the previous night at a large men's shelter where the author has a permanent bed. Long recordings follow from a six-lane boulevard median, where he holds a donation sign and repeats aloud, word for word, the statements he says are directed at passing vehicles.

Early in the month he changes his sign to recommend searching for a book about neurotechnology and mental privacy. After that, the recordings report a new vehicle statement alongside one that had stayed the same for a year. From 13 November they also capture unidentified groups that the author says are "overriding" the vehicle statements, changing their wording in real time. By 21 November these sessions make some recordings three times their usual length.

## Recurring statements

After the sign change they add a statement telling drivers not to search for that information (IS-J04-20251113-R04), sometimes with a threat of punishment. Drivers who respond are said to be added to a "euthanization list" (IS-J04-20251111-R03, IS-J04-20251129-R01).
- **Euthanization and "ugly workflows".** Repeated requests that the author accept euthanization. The statements describe a list, a "cleanup crew", financial suppression and a money motive. On 13 and 14 November, statements discuss shooting the author (IS-J04-20251113-R03, IS-J04-20251113-R04, IS-J04-20251114-R03).
- **Overrides by other groups.** Altered statements such as "This is not the spirit world. We're human beings." (IS-J04-20251113-R05). Speakers say they are documenting this, some with funding (IS-J04-20251114-R08, IS-J04-20251119-R04, IS-J04-20251121-R05).
