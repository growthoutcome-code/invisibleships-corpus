## What the terms file says, in brief

- **Statements are not the author's views.** Transcript statements are external communications. The archive says it accuses no named person, company or government body, and that all of it requires independent verification (IS-META-TERMS).
- **Statements are testimony.** A statement in the transcripts is a first-person report, verified by nobody. It moves into the research record only if a document, ruling or public record independently corroborates it.
- **Naming is not accusing.** Where an organisation is named, the file says it is named only because a source said the name, or because a public record contains it.
- **Four limits:** nothing establishes that anything specific was done to a specific person; no capability is documented that reads perception at a distance; an absence of evidence is only an absence; and sources placed side by side do not corroborate one another.
- **Physical symptoms** reported alongside the record are described as "testimony twice over", and the file places no cause beside any symptom. It also says that anyone experiencing such symptoms should see a doctor, because they have ordinary, treatable causes too.
- **Sharing:** the complete corpus may be shared, and quoting with attribution is permitted. Altered republication and commercial use without permission are not.
