## What it contains
**October 2024 to June 2025 — typed entries only.** The author writes timestamped logs of the statements he hears, tagged by the apparent speaker and often marked as paraphrasing. The densest stretches are 4–8 March, late May and 8–11 June. The places are first an apartment where he was a guest, then, from mid-May, a shelter, coffee shops and resource centres.

**July 2025 — the method changes.** On 2 July he describes statements heard as audible sound in street noise. From 10 July he repeats the statements aloud into a phone recorder and transcribes them with an AI tool. He records at an intersection where he holds a donation sign, on walks and outside shelters. July is the largest month in Part 01 (about 105,000 words).

**August 2025 — audio becomes the norm.** From 4 August most material is recorded and transcribed. Most recordings are from the street intersection, and the author closes days with his own summaries and counts. Part 01 ends on 22 August, when the Google Doc reached its length limit and Part 02 began.

- Promises of extraction or witness protection, with no in-person contact recorded.
