## What this month contains
This is the first month in Part 01 with audio. Each entry is a short day page with a "Methodology" and "Scene" note and a list of that day's recordings. The author describes the method: he repeats aloud what he hears while recording on his phone, and an AI service transcribes the audio (IS-J01-20250804-ENTRY). Recordings alternate between a "repeating process" (statements spoken back as heard, often followed by "that's exactly what I said, word for word"), a "listening process" (silent gaps followed by the author's summary), and long "administrative notes" in which the author speaks for himself. Much of the text, especially from 12 August on, is administrative notes rather than repeated statements; most recordings are marked as paraphrasing.

Recording took place on walks across Denver, around a large homeless shelter where the author was staying, and at a street intersection where he stood with a cardboard sign, which the entries call the "battle for the intersection" (IS-J01-20250812-ENTRY onward). Activity is concentrated on 12 August (6 recordings) and 22 August (10 recordings).
