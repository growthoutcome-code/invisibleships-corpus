## brain-data-as-a-service ([brain.space](http://brain.space))

: The first end-to-end EEG system from [brain.space](http://brain.space) that extracts, labels and analyzes data in real time. The perfect solution for the researcher EEG needs. Proprietary hardware: automated process non-invasively and accurately collects brain data with the click of a button. Brain Data is de-noised, standardized and automatically tagged by ML models. Analytic tools provide two levels of Brain Data analysis: clean data for research and Brain Insights.

*—*[*Brain.Space*](http://brain.space)
