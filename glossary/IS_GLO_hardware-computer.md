## hardware (computer)

hard·​ware,    HARD-wair,   *noun*

: the physical components (such as electronic and electrical devices) of a vehicle (such as a spacecraft) or an apparatus (such as a computer)

Example:

“But more recently, some companies have been working toward mating AI technology with hardware.”

*—Alan Stafford, Forbes.com, 1 May 2025*
