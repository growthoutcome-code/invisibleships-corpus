## perceptual set

hard·​ware,    HARD-wair,   *noun*

A Perceptual Set is a tendency to perceive or notice some aspects of the available sensory data and ignore others. It is heavily influenced by expectations, past experience and context. If an object is completely outside a person's prior experience (their "set"), they may not select it for conscious attention, effectively rendering it "invisible" to their mind's eye, even if their retinas physically register it.
