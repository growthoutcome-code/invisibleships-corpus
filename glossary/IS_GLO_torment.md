## torment

tormented; tormenting; torments

tor·​ment, TOR-ment,  *transistive verb*

: to cause severe, usually persistent or recurrent distress of the body or mind

Examples: 

“The cattle were tormented by flies.”

“He was tormented by doubt.”

“She liked me, but she laughed at me, and tormented me…” *- Charles Dickens*

*—Merriam Webster Dictionary*
