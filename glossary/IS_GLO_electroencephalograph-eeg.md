## electroencephalograph (EEG)

elec·​tro·​en·​ceph·​a·​lo·​graph,   uh-leck-troh-uhn-SEFF-uh-luh-graff,   *noun*

: an apparatus for detecting and recording brain waves

Example:

“As the mice slept, the researchers watched the rodents’ brain activity on an electroencephalograph, or EEG, and recorded the animals’ blood pressures and heart and respiratory rates.”

*—Roni Dengler, Discover Magazine, 27 Feb. 2019*
