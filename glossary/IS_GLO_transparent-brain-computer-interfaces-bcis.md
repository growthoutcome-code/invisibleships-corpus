## transparent brain-computer interfaces (BCIs)

: Transparent Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs) utilize nanotechnology to create devices that are both functional and transparent, allowing for observation of the underlying brain tissue during operation. This transparency is crucial for research and may offer advantages in future clinical applications.
