## telepathic

tel·e·path·ic,    tel-uh-PATH-ik,   *adjective*

: of or relating to telepathy

: having the ability to know another person's thoughts without being told what they are : able to read minds

Example:

“You need to tell me what you're thinking. I'm not telepathic.”
