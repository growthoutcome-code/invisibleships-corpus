any chart or brief was made. The site renders a selection of this; the
whole input is here so the findings can be rebuilt and checked rather
than taken on trust.

These are working files. They carry the same terms as everything else
in this corpus — see `meta/IS_META_terms.md`. Evidence tiers apply:
a row present here is not, by itself, a verified fact.
