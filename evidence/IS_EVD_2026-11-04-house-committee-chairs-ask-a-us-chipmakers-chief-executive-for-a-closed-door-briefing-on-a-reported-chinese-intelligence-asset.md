## Captured text

November 4, 2026 
 
Mr. Jensen Huang 
Founder & CEO 
NVIDIA Corporation 
2788 San Tomas Expressway 
Santa Clara, CA 95051 
 
Dear Mr. Huang:  
 
We are writing to request that you appear before our committees for a joint, closed- door briefing 
on NVIDIA’s security protocols against foreign adversary espionage and your interactions with a 
publicly reported People’s Republic of China (PRC) intelligence asset: Megaspeed executive Alice 
Huang.  
 
Recent reports raise significant national security concerns regarding the PRC’s access to sensitive 
U.S. technology and information. The issue of PRC infiltration and access to cutting- edge U.S. 
technology is core to the investigative work of our three com mittees this Congress. It is vital that 
Congress determines the extent to which U.S. national security has been placed at risk.   
 
We formally request that you appear  in room 2172 of the Rayburn House Office Building on 
November 18, 2026, to brief our committees. 
 
We request acceptance of this date for your appearance or the provision of an alternative date that 
is during a session day of the 119th Congress by November 11, 2026.  
 
 
Sincerely, 
 
 
 
 
 
Brian J. Mast 
Chairman 
House Committee on 
Foreign Affairs  
 
 
 
 
John Moolenaar 
Chairman 
House Select Committee 
on the CCP 
 
 
 
 
 
Eric A. “Rick” Crawford 
Chairman 
House Permanent Select 
Committee on Intelligence 
BRIAN J. MAST, FLORIDA  
CHAIRMAN 
 
 
JAMES LANGENDERFER  
STAFF DIRECTOR 
 
 
One Hundred Nineteenth Congress
