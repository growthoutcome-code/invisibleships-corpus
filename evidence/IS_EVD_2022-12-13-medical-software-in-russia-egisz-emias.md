## 2025: Ministry of Health of the Russian Federation: Systems for analyzing CT and MRI images of Siemens and Agfa replaced with software from Rostelecom, Rostec and Makhaon

The ministry [health care](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Health_care) [Russia](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Russia) reported on the completion of import substitution of foreign [software](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Software) for the analysis of medical images, replacing the systems [Siemens](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Siemens) with [Agfa](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Company:Agfa) domestic solutions "," [Rostelecom](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Rostelecom)"" and [Rostec](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Rostec)"." [Swallowtail](https://tadviser.com/index.php?title=Company:Mahaon_Group&action=edit&redlink=1) This was announced by the Director [Ministry of Health](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Ministry_of_Health) [Dmitry Temnov](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Person:Temnov_Dmitry_Vladimirovich) of the Department of Digital Development and IT in July 2025.

Substitution occurred in the field of radiological systems and systems for the analysis of medical images of [computed tomography](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Article:Computed_tomography) and magnetic resonance imaging. Russian products have completely supplanted foreign solutions from the country's medical institutions.

In the field of laboratory information systems for clinical laboratories and analyzers, solutions from [Alice](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Company:Alice), [Bars](https://tadviser.com/index.php?title=Bars_Group&action=edit&redlink=1) Group and Rostelecom have [been](https://tadviser.com/index.php?title=Bars_Group&action=edit&redlink=1) introduced. The transition to domestic software covered medical information systems, hospital and laboratory complexes.

Temnov noted that the industrial competence center "Healthcare," unlike other ICCs, directed efforts not to import substitution of software, but to create niche solutions. Analogues of such products exist only in a limited number of foreign developers.

The main task is the advanced development of medical technologies. Many developments use artificial intelligence algorithms, and the Ministry of Health is working on accelerated registration of AI-enabled products.

The demonstration day of the Healthcare ICC showed that the Russian medical IT industry has moved from the stage of forced import substitution to the phase of generating innovations. Particular attention is paid to the development of artificial intelligence and telemedicine.

The Ministry of Health relies on the support of technological startups to ensure the advanced development of medical technologies. The agency seeks to create conditions for the introduction of innovative solutions in practical healthcare.[\[1\]](https://tadviser.com#cite_note-0)

## 2024: The medical data market size in Russia reached 4.2 billion rubles

At the end of 2024, the confirmed volume of the medical data market in [Russia](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Russia) amounted to about 1.9 billion [rubles](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Russian_ruble). Taking into account the internal IT projects of organizations, decisions in the field of artificial intelligence and other initiatives, the figure reaches 4.2 billion rubles. Such data are contained in the review of the audit and consulting company [Kept](<https://tadviser.com/index.php/Company:Kept_-_Cap_(formerly_KPMG)>), published on October 7, 2025.

The published conclusions are based on the results of a comprehensive survey, which was attended by medical organizations, pharmaceutical companies, laboratories, government agencies, enterprises for the production of medical equipment, universities, national medical research centers, specialized institutions, etc. The study was carried out with the support of the Association of Pharmaceutical Companies ["Pharmaceutical Innovations"](<https://tadviser.com/index.php?title=InPharma_(Pharmaceutical_Innovation)&action=edit&redlink=1>) (Infarma), the Association of International Pharmaceutical Manufacturers (AIPM), [the Alliance in the field of artificial intelligence](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Company:AI-Russia_Alliance), the Big [Data Association](<https://tadviser.com/index.php?title=Big_Data_Association_(Big_Data_Association)&action=edit&redlink=1>) and [the ANO "Digital Economy."](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Company:ANO_Digital_Economy)

Medical data, including real-world clinical practice (RCP) information, represent a significant resource for digital healthcare transformation, analysts point [out](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Health_care). They can be used to improve the quality of management decision-making, budget planning, and cost reduction, evaluation, and efficiency of patient care. At the national level, the exchange of this data contributes to the implementation of government initiatives, such as the Data Economics project, as well as the development of [pharmaceuticals](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Pharmaceutics) and the medical industry, reducing R&D costs, etc.

The study says that pharmaceutical companies are key consumers of medical data in Russia: they account for up to 90% of the total market capacity. The rest is shared by other players, including [AI-based](https://tadviser.com/index.php/AI) solution developers, manufacturers of medical devices and equipment, etc. [State](https://tadviser.com/index.php?title=State_and_social_structures&action=edit&redlink=1) structures are the operator of a large array of data, but do not participate in their commercial circulation. A significant part of medical information is publicly available (for example, data on the incidence of population and radiographic data) and is used by industry participants free of charge.

In the future, the market is expected to continue to show growth, which will be facilitated by overcoming current barriers (including regulatory ones), the emergence of new players, the strengthening of the role of digital technologies and the increasing need to use data to make more effective management and medical decisions. Analysts highlight several key trends:

- Improving the quality, structuring and standardization of information;
- The development of new tools for the analysis of medical data with the aim of developing a personalized approach in treatment and achieving marketing and management goals;
- Reorienting the market towards RCP research;
- Generating interest in data from new players against the background of dissatisfaction with current demand (demand for information exceeds its supply);
- Active dissemination of new types of data, including digital patient profiles, as well as information generated by remote monitoring of the condition;
-  An increase in the number of private medical organizations registering electronic medical documents with the Uniform State Health Information System State Information System (Unified State Information System in the Field of Health), and an increase in the volume of data transmitted by them.<sup>[\[2\]](https://tadviser.com#cite_note-1)</sup>

## 2022: Roszdravnadzor makes it easier to update healthcare IT solutions

In December 2022, it became known about the decision of Roszdravnadzor to simplify the update [of](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Roszdravnadzor) IT solutions in the field of [healthcare](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Health_care). This is about making it easier to make changes to software registered as a medical product.

The regulator allowed to refine [the software](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Software), provided that its profile and purpose are preserved. The agency will automatically check the sensitivity and specificity of [software](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Software) on datacets that were used in clinical trials of the product with a certain frequency. The indicators after the software update should be no worse than when the product was originally registered.

|  | As for the problem of rapid software product change: we see a solution related to the approach that it does not matter what changes have occurred in the software product, it is important for us that it works no worse. Of course, if the purpose of the software product is not replaced, if we do not switch from X-rays to images of a different kind or analysis of other data. In this case, of course, an assessment will be required at the entrance to the market, - said [Dmitry Pavlyukov](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Person:Dmitry_Yuryevich_Pavlyukov), deputy head of Roszdravnadzor. |  |

## 2020

### The Ministry of Health of the Russian Federation has updated the requirements for the documentation of medical software

On December 21, 2020, the Ministry of [Justice of the Russian](<https://tadviser.com/index.php/Company:Ministry_of_Justice_of_the_Russian_Federation_(Ministry_of_Justice)>) Federation registered an order of the [Ministry of](<https://tadviser.com/index.php/Company:Ministry_of_Justice_of_the_Russian_Federation_(Ministry_of_Justice)>) [Health](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Ministry_of_Health) of [the Russian](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Software) [Federation](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Ministry_of_Health), which, among other things, defines new requirements for documentation of [medical](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Medicine) [software](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Software). We are talking about amendments to the profile order No. 11n of January 19, 2017, which will enter into force on January 1, 2021.

The main change is the emergence of a new chapter II, which is called: "Requirements for the content of the technical documentation of the manufacturer (manufacturer) for software that is a medical device." It contains 24 requirements, starting with the name of [the software](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Software) and ending with "information about the initial release or the latest revision of the technical documentation for the software."

Among them are items on the purpose of the medical device, the source of the dataset, which are used as an algorithm, description and characteristics of the program, technical and hardware requirements, information on the order of installation and removal, versions of the software and ways to update it, as well as requirements for the qualification of persons for installing and using the software.

The document also talks about protection against unauthorized interference, assessment of risks and vulnerabilities, ways to protect against them, including antivirus programs, multi-level access and cryptographic protection.

Based on the explanations given by [Roszdravnadzor](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Roszdravnadzor), the software is considered a medical product if it is intended for:

- control of equipment operation and monitoring of its functioning;
- receipt of diagnostic data from the equipment, their accumulation and calculation in automatic mode;
- monitoring of human body functions and transmission of the obtained data (including through wireless technologies);
- calculation of dose selection parameters (irradiation, drug, radiopaque substance, etc.);
- processing data obtained from diagnostic medical equipment, transferring it to planning and therapy systems;
- processing of medical images (including changes in their quality, color resolution, etc.).<sup>[\[3\]](https://tadviser.com#cite_note-2)</sup>

### The criteria by which software in Russia will be classified as medical devices have been changed

On February 14, 2020, [Roszdravnadzor](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Roszdravnadzor) changed the criteria for classifying [medical](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Medicine) [software](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Software) as medical devices. This follows from the information message of the department posted on its website. [download](http://www.tadviser.ru/index.php/%D0%A4%D0%B0%D0%B9%D0%BB:2_5213391055193376572.pdf)

Roszdravnadzor announced the cancellation of its information letter "On Registration of Software," created on December 30, 2015. [\[1\]](<http://www.tadviser.ru/index.php/%D0%A4%D0%B0%D0%B9%D0%BB:1452588601.37533-1-17032(1).pdf>) At the same time, the agency informs about the posting on its website of another letter, "On Software," dated February 13, 2020.

It, as noted by Roszdravnadzor, contains the position of the commission of its subordinate institution, the [All-Russian Research and Testing Institute of Medical Technology (VNIIIMT)](<https://tadviser.com/index.php/Company:All-Russian_Research_and_Testing_Institute_of_Medical_Technology_(VNIIIMT)>), on issuing conclusions to requests related to the circulation of medical devices. The letter also contains the position of the institution on the classification of software to medical devices, including the exact criteria for such a ranking.

As explained to TAdviser in the association of developers and users of [artificial intelligence](<https://tadviser.com/index.php/Artificial_intelligence_(AI,_Artificial_intelligence,_AI)>) ["National Base of Medical Knowledge"](<https://tadviser.com/index.php?title=National_Medical_Knowledge_Base_(NBMZ)&action=edit&redlink=1>) (NBMZ), software that is a medical device is subject to technical and clinical tests, examination and subsequent [state](https://tadviser.com/index.php/State) registration. The association also noted that the letter "On software registration," created in 2015, also contained criteria for classifying software as medical devices, but they were abstract.

The position of the VNIIIMT Institute, contained in the information letter "On Software," compiled in 2020, is as follows. A medical device is a software if:

- is a program for a computer or its modules, regardless of the hardware platform used, as well as methods of placing software and providing access to it;


- is not part of another medical device;


- intended by the manufacturer for medical care;


- The result of the software is an automatic interpretation.


In particular, interpretation can use artificial intelligence technology, as well as a set of data obtained from medical devices admitted to circulation in accordance with the established procedure or entered by [medical](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Health_care) professionals to provide appropriate assistance. In addition, the interpretation may correspond to the parameters set by the health care professional that influence clinical decision-making.

They are not interpretations of data, for example, such methods and technologies used in software:

- display of data received from a medical device, including in a given format;


- calculation by given formulas;


- Translation between units


- construction of statistical reports and graphs;


- Raster or Vector Image Editor


- alarm of deviations in data if possible;


- display of initial data and subject to setting parameters;


- alarm of deviations by the user;


-  Create screen forms, [business processes](https://tadviser.com/index.php/BPM), reporting, and other views that are used to automate the medical organization's business processes during software operation.


As examples of software that is a medical device, the institute cites the following products:

-  Software for the doctor to view an individual anatomical ZD model based on [computed tomography](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Article:Computed_tomography) images;


- Software to support medical decision-making in case of stroke;


- Software to help the doctor diagnose malignant neoplasms;


- Software to help a doctor diagnose tuberculosis or viral meningitis in children;


- Medical Decision Support for Colorectal Cancer Risk


- Software to help the doctor diagnose arrhythmia;


- Software to assist the doctor in planning the technique of surgical operations;


- Software to assist the doctor in performing morphometric measurements;


- Remote health monitoring software for elderly patients with comorbid chronic diseases;


- Software for the development of an individual rehabilitation program;


- Software used as prescribed by a physician to calculate by a diabetic patient at high risk of hypoglycemia a bolus dose of insulin based on carbohydrate intake, expected physical activity, and pre-meal blood glucose levels;


- Software of the radiological system for archiving and transmitting images for obtaining, storing, transmitting, processing and viewing images by a doctor.


As examples of non-medical device software, the institute designates developments such as:

- Software designed to automate the administrative and economic activities of a medical organization;


- Software, including mobile applications designed by the manufacturer to promote a healthy lifestyle and create a responsible attitude in people to preserve and promote health, maintain active longevity, which measures/calculates the number of steps, walking/running speed, pulse, number of calories/fluids spent and/or consumed, weight, body mass index, etc.


- medical information systems of a medical organization, laboratory ICs, electronic medical record management software, image archiving and transmission systems, if such software does not contain data interpretation functions;


- Software, including its update, used to manage the medical device and monitor its operability;


- Software that uses data from one or more medical devices but is not intended to provide medical care. For example, software that encrypts and/or combines data obtained from one or more medical devices for further transmission;


- Software for the exchange of text and/or voice messages, electronic documents, photographic images, video, audio recordings/streams and other data between a medical professional and a patient, their registration, storage and access to them in the provision of medical care, including using telemedicine technologies, or for appointment;


- Software for accounting, planning and monitoring of routine maintenance and scheduled repair of medical devices;


- Software for an unlimited range of users, which has educational, popular science, reference and information purposes, including the choice of a medical specialist.


The NBMZ TAdviser association said that the set of criteria relating software to medical devices is clear and transparent.

|  | If the software is intended by the manufacturer for the provision of medical care and if this software somehow interprets the medical information submitted to it, then such software should be referred to as a medical device. If there are no such signs, then this is the most common software and it is not necessary to register it as a medical device. For the market, this is of great importance, - noted in the NBMZ. |  | 

As an example of the positive effect of the innovation in the association, the fact was cited that, thanks to it, ordinary electronic medical record management systems, medical information systems, electronic registrars ceased to be in some cases a medical device. Namely, when they lack the functions of supporting medical decision-making, as well as do not form hints to a medical professional and do not interpret the information entered.

The association also drew attention to the fact that from the concept of "medical device," in particular, all administrative and economic software, reference books and mobile applications for patients on a healthy lifestyle are made.

## 2019

### The issue of simplifying the registration of medical software does not shift from the dead center

To resolve issues related to medical [software](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Software), three working groups will be created at the initiative of the [Federal Service for Supervision of Healthcare](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Roszdravnadzor) (Rosszdravnadzor). They will help to take into account the opinion of all stakeholders when resolving industry issues, the National Medical [Knowledge Base](<https://tadviser.com/index.php?title=National_Medical_Knowledge_Base_(NBMZ)&action=edit&redlink=1>) (NBMZ) [told](<https://tadviser.com/index.php?title=National_Medical_Knowledge_Base_(NBMZ)&action=edit&redlink=1>) TAdviser in October 2019 at the Association of Developers and Users of Artificial Intelligence Systems in [Medicine.](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Medicine)

As follows from the minutes of the industry regulatory workshop held by Rossdravnadzor on October 17, which TAdviser reviewed, the following working groups will be established:

-  on the issues of establishing criteria for classifying [software](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Software) as medical devices, as well as their classification by classes of potential risk of use and types;
- on the issues of forming requirements for the technical and operational documentation of medical software, as well as issues of conducting technical and clinical trials;
- on improving the procedure for registering medical software (software).


Roszdravnadzor expects from "interested parties" the proposal of candidates for inclusion in the working groups. The agency accepts proposals until October 25. The NBMZ clarified to TAdviser that all three created groups will carry out their work under Roszdravnadzor.

In the direction of improving the procedure for registering medical software, the industry, as well as the Skolkovo Foundation, is actively working. The NBMZ Association and [Skolkovo](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Skolkovo_Innovation_Center) propose to simplify the state registration of medical software without registering it as a medical device.

There may be different options for this simplification. Thus, Skolkovo, together with the Center for Competence in Regulatory Regulation, the Pepeliaev [Group](https://tadviser.com/index.php?title=Pepeliaev_Group&action=edit&redlink=1) Law Firm and with the participation of the NBMZ Association, has developed a draft Federal Law on Amendments to the Federal Law "On the Basics of Protecting the Health of Citizens." He proposes to introduce the concept of medical software (MPO), a special procedure for its [state](https://tadviser.com/index.php/State) registration and the ability not to subject it to some types of software included in the MPO and that software that is not it.

To date, as TAdviser was told in the NBMZ, the bill was considered at the working group [of the ANO "Digital Economy"](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Company:ANO_Digital_Economy) and, in accordance with its decision, is additionally being worked out by the Competence Center for Regulatory Regulation with authorized federal executive bodies of the Russian Federation (FOIV).

Business is an active supporter of the adoption of the bill. Among the companies supporting the bill are [Sberbank](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Sberbank_of_the_Russian_Federation), USM Group, [Megafon,](https://tadviser.com/index.php/MegaFon) [1C,](https://tadviser.com/index.php?title=1C_Joint_Stock_Company&action=edit&redlink=1) [MTS,](<https://tadviser.com/index.php/Company:Mobile_TeleSystems_(MTS)>) [Post Modern Technologies.](<https://tadviser.com/index.php?title=Post_Modern_Technologies_(PMT)&action=edit&redlink=1>)

|  | The same companies that are not his supporters are some of those who have already conducted their medical software through the state registration, which is laid for medical devices. They argue that since they managed to adapt to it, it may not be necessary to make the exceptions proposed by the bill, "the NBMZ told Tadviser. |  | 

As for the Ministry of [Health of the Russian Federation](<https://tadviser.com/index.php/Company:Ministry_of_Health_of_the_Russian_Federation_(Ministry_of_Health)>), the association told TAdviser that the department refuses to support the bill.
