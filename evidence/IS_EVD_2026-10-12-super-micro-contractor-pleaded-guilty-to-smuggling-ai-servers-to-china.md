## Captured text

Sun also arranged for fake "dummy" servers to be placed on-site in December 2025 and lied to a U.S. Department of Commerce official who came to inspect units that had in fact already been shipped to China, according to the indictment. According to authorities, he and his co-conspirators funneled Nvidia-powered servers through a Southeast Asian intermediary, which ultimately delivered them to end customers in China. The indictment named Nvidia's B200, H100, and H200 graphics processing units, and servers containing them, as subject to U.S. export restrictions.
