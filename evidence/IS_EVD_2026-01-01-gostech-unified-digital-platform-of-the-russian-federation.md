### 2026

#### New name and special procedure for GIS location. In Russia, adopted the law on "Gostekh"

On May 26, 2026 [State Duma](https://tadviser.com/index.php/State_Duma) [RUSSIAN FEDERATION](https://tadviser.com/index.php/RUSSIAN_FEDERATION) , it adopted the law "On the technological platform for the creation, development and operation of information systems." Previously, the document was called "On the unified digital platform" [of the Russian Federation](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Russia) Gosteh. "

The law establishes the organizational and legal basis for the creation, development and operation of state, municipal and other information systems on a technological platform. It will include information technologies, databases, technical, software, software and hardware and other means provided, including through information and telecommunication networks.

Russia
[Ministry of Digital Development](<https://tadviser.com/index.php/Project:Ministry_of_Digital_Development,_Communications_and_Mass_Media_of_the_Russian_Federation_(Ministry_of_Digital_Development)>), as an authorized body, will ensure the provision of components to the participants of the technological platform for the creation, development and operation of information systems or other technological support for the activities of the platform participants, interaction between the owners of the platform components and its participants. It is possible to transfer on the proposal of the Ministry of Digital Development of Russia certain powers of the organization determined by the Government [of the](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Russia) Russian Federation. Participants of the technology platform may be:

- [State authorities](https://tadviser.com/index.php?title=State_and_social_structures&action=edit&redlink=1) of the Russian Federation;
- State authorities of the constituent entities of the Russian Federation;
- Other government agencies formed in accordance with the law;
- Public authorities of federal territories;
- Local governments;
-  Management bodies of [state](https://tadviser.com/index.php?title=State_and_social_structures&action=edit&redlink=1) extra-budgetary funds;
- Other persons authorized in accordance with regulatory legal acts to take measures to create, develop and operate information systems;
- Commercial organizations, non-profit organizations, in respect of which the government commission, on the proposal of the authorized body, decided to admit such organizations as participants in the technological platform.


The new regulation is expected to optimize the processes of information processing in information systems and the use of [software](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Software) and hardware.[\[1\]](https://tadviser.com#cite_note-0)

#### 38 GIS work at Gostekh instead of 150, which were planned by the government

Typical cloud solutions available on the unified Gostech digital platform have been tested by more than 25 Russian regions. This was announced at the end of May 2026 by the Deputy Prime Minister of the Russian [Federation](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Governments_of_the_Russian_Federation) - Head of the Government Apparatus [Dmitry Grigorenko](https://tadviser.com/index.php?title=Person:Dmitry_Yuryevich_Grigorenko&action=edit&redlink=1).

According to him, the regions are provided with access to safe and ready-to-implement digital services - from public services and systems for processing citizens' appeals to industry solutions in the social sphere, [education](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Education), etc. At the same time, the platform provides the necessary [IT infrastructure](https://tadviser.com/index.php/IT_infrastructure), which allows you to speed up the launch of new services, reduce costs, and increase the stability and security of government InformSystems.

|  | Typical IT solutions on the GosTech platform form a single digital standard for the whole country. This approach helps the regions to quickly implement convenient digital services for citizens and businesses. At the same time, it does not require direct budgetary costs from the regions, due to which the costs of developing IT systems are reduced, says Grigorenko. |  | 

In addition to regional solutions, 38 state information systems (GIS) are deployed on the Gostech platform, which are used by the Ministry of Emergencies, the [Russian](https://tadviser.com/index.php/MINISTRY_OF_EMERGENCY_SITUATIONS) [Guard](https://tadviser.com/index.php/National_Guard_of_the_Russian_Federation), the Ministry of [Education and Science, the](<https://tadviser.com/index.php/Company:Ministry_of_Education_and_Science_of_the_Russian_Federation_(Ministry_of_Education_and_Science)>) Ministry of [Natural Resources](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Ministry_of_Natural_Resources), the Ministry of [Agriculture](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Ministry_of_Agriculture) and other federal authorities. However, the number of such GIS is significantly less than the planned value. In particular, in 2023, Prime Minister [Mikhail Mishustin](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Person:Mishustin_Mikhail_Vladimirovich) announced that 150 of the most socially significant GIS would be transferred to Gostech as a priority, while it was planned to complete these works by 2024. Thus, in fact, only 25% of the planned number of information systems were launched on the platform. At the same time, as of May 2026, five typical cloud solutions were implemented at Gostekh - in the fields of social protection, trade, advertising, burial management and veterinary medicine.[\[2\]](https://tadviser.com#cite_note-1)

#### The bill on GosTech has been submitted to the State Duma. The market reacted ambiguously

On the twentieth of November, the Government of the Russian Federation submitted to the [State Duma](https://tadviser.com/index.php/State_Duma) Bill No. 1076648-8 "On the Unified Digital Platform of the Russian Federation 'GosTech'<sup>[\[4\]](https://tadviser.com#cite_note-3)</sup>which was registered and sent for consideration to the Committee on Information Policy, Information Technology and Communications.

|  | The proposed regulatory regulation will optimize the processes of information processing in information systems and the use of software and software and hardware, - said in an explanatory note to the bill. |  | 

The text of the bill says that it will enter into force 90 days after publication. In addition, the accompanying documentation states that it will require the development of the following by-laws:

- Provisions on the GosTech platform;
- Procedure for interaction between the state functional customer and the state technical customer;
- Standard form of the agreement on the distribution of responsibility between the participant and the coordinator of the GosTech platform;
- Requirements that the services of the GosTech platform must meet.


These four documents will have to be developed by [the Ministry of Digital Development](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Ministry_of_Digital_Development) within the same 90 days from the date of publication of the bill. In addition, the [FSB of Russia](https://tadviser.com/index.php/FSB_of_Russia) is ordered to develop a procedure for the continuous interaction of the coordinator of the unified digital platform "GosTech" with [State system of detection, prevention and elimination of consequences of computer attacks of](https://tadviser.com/index.php/State_system_of_detection,_prevention_and_elimination_of_consequences_of_computer_attacks) the same period.

|  | The bill was developed in order to regulate the legal status of the GosTech platform, determine the powers and relationships between its participants, as well as consolidate the requirements for infrastructure, reliability, resiliency and information security, "Igor Tyukachev, head of business development at [Indid](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Indid), explained to TAdviser. - In fact, it forms uniform rules for the platform and sets standards that all services provided must meet. This is important since the GosTech platform was created as a large-scale state cloud platform, to which various departmental systems should gradually switch. |  | 

The bill defines the following roles in relation to the GosTech platform:

- The coordinator of the GosTech platform is the federal body responsible for IT. It's Ministry of Digital Development;
- Participants of the GosTech platform are its users;
- The operators of the GosTech platform services are organizations that ensure the functioning of the platform hardware and software.


The participants of the platform can be state authorities of the Russian Federation, subjects and local self-government, as well as other state bodies. Of the organizations, the platform services can be used by state extra-budgetary funds and commercial companies - state corporations, state-owned companies, state funds, state and municipal institutions.

Now the operator of the GosTech platform is the State Technologies subordinate to the Ministry of Digital Development of federal state institution (PKU [GosTech](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Company:State_Technologies_PKU_Gosteh)), the role of which seems to be changing. In particular, a separate paragraph of the bill states that the coordinator, by decision of the government, can entrust the functions of admission to the platform, ensuring interaction with it, development and control to the "federal state institution subordinate to it." Most likely, this will be done by PKU "GosTech" in the future. However, this should finally be enshrined in the by-laws listed above.

The operators under the new bill will be commercial companies, which, as stated in the text, will "have on the right of ownership, lease or on another legal basis technical and software-technical means, data processing centers and complexes of technical means... and providing the technical ability to provide services of the GosTech platform." As a result, the participants of the GosTech platform are removed from the obligation to independently buy [hardware and software](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Software) - it is enough for them to purchase only operator services through a coordinator.

|  | The main advantage of the platform is centralization and standardization, "Ivan Shumovsky, Deputy Director of the Clouds and Data Department of [Reksoft](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Reksoft), told TAdviser. - The development of new systems with an eye on Gostech can be significantly cheaper than when each department develops solutions "for itself," which is especially important in conditions of budget deficit. In addition, the platform technologically "pulls up" small and regional departments, which often do not have the resources to maintain a staff of highly qualified IT specialists. |  | 

According to the expert, centralized services can be deployed on a single platform much more efficiently and faster, for example, on the basis of [artificial intelligence](<https://tadviser.com/index.php/Artificial_intelligence_(AI,_Artificial_intelligence,_AI)>) - AI assistants for [public services](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Public_services). This will create more advanced and better supported services for everyone.

|  | In fact, the GosTech law is aimed at combining fragmented state IT into a single, standardized circuit with clearly defined procedures and regulations, "said Artem Fomichev, commercial director of [Giant Computer Systems](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Company:Giant_Computer_Systems). - For citizens and businesses on the surface, this looks good: disparate interfaces will take on a general look, and new services will appear faster, because departments do not have to invent their own bike every time. |  | 

IT companies, according to the expert, are also interested in legislating the rules, since participation in the platform becomes formalized, roles, statuses, paid admission for commercial players appear - along with an understandable integration market for a single standard. However, there are also disadvantages: if GosTech falls, everything falls. The risk of major incidents increases simply because of scale. And unique systems that used to live on their own are now forced to adapt to the general template - flexibility falls, the input threshold grows.

|  | When we talk about information security, we must understand: if the platform was originally designed taking into account all the requirements of information security, its components are updated in a timely manner, security audits are carried out and the identified shortcomings are eliminated, then such a service will be much more reliable than ten different platforms with different contractors for their maintenance, - Dmitry [Ovchinnikov](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Person:Dmitry_2_Ovchinnikov), architect of information security and vCISO UserGate, explained to TAdviser [readers](https://tadviser.com/index.php/UserGate). - Centralization of the deployment of digital services will allow the use of uniform information security templates, which will be the same for all contractors and representatives of state and municipal authorities, which will lead to increased data security and ensure the reliable operation of government services. |  | 

Indeed, already at the level of the bill there is a clause on compliance with all legislative requirements for the protection of state information systems, personal data processing systems and [critical information infrastructure](https://tadviser.com/index.php/CUES). As already mentioned, GosTech operators will have to ensure continuous interaction with State system of detection, prevention and elimination of consequences of computer attacks, which should strengthen control over the information security of the platform and individual operators from the [state](https://tadviser.com/index.php/State). Nevertheless, experts surveyed by TAdviser still have doubts about the effectiveness of the measures taken.

Depends on the sufficiency and flexibility of the component - at the moment they are not enough. And public statements that the platform is waiting for all market participants cause distrust. |  | 

#### The government agreed on a bill on the use of the Gostech platform

In the twentieth of November 2025, the Government of the Russian Federation approved the draft federal law on the unified digital platform "Gosteh." The document fixes the initiative in the legal field, and also lays down a single conceptual apparatus for its further development, including within the framework of the Gostech 2.0 project.

According to the Kommersant newspaper, earlier [the](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Kommersant) functioning of Gostekh was regulated only by presidential decree (No. 231 of March 31, 2023) and regulatory acts. The adopted document for the first time enshrines the use of the platform at the level of federal legislation.

The goal of Gostech is to speed up and reduce the cost of creating state information systems (GIS), while ensuring their compatibility, reliability and security (thanks to centralized control). The platform allows you to develop and develop services using cloud technologies, ready-made components and uniform standards. In addition, Gostech is considered as one of the tools of the digital ecosystem of public administration. The development of the platform is supervised by Deputy Prime Minister [Dmitry Grigorenko](https://tadviser.com/index.php?title=Person:Dmitry_Yuryevich_Grigorenko&action=edit&redlink=1).

|  | The new model of legal regulation of the platform legislatively creates uniform standards for the development and operation of GIS, forms a more transparent, secure and scalable architecture, the Grigorenko apparatus said. |  | 

Sources [of Kommersant](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Company:ID_Kommersant) note that the approved bill is the first example in [Russia](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Russia) of regulating cloud technologies: we are talking about the subsequent transition of information systems, including GIS, to a cloud model. The adoption of the document is expected to create a market for service providers (service operators) within the platform and will make it possible to use Gostech to business (it will be able to supply services and be a customer of services).[\[5\]](https://tadviser.com#cite_note-4)

#### The Ministry of Digital Development has prepared a document on the regulation of the work of "Gostekha"

[The Ministry of Digital Development](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Ministry_of_Digital_Development) has developed amendments to the law "On Information Technologies and Information Protection," which will regulate the operation of the [Gostech](https://tadviser.com/index.php/State_that) platform. The bill was agreed with the government commission on legislative activities. This became known in November 2025.

According to [Vedomosti](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Company:Sheets), Gostech is a single digital platform on which federal and regional bodies will be able to quickly create state systems and digital services. The site is a cloud platform. The goal was to simplify the interaction of Russians and [the state](https://tadviser.com/index.php/State).

Changes in legislation will consolidate the position of Gostekha as a single digital platform for creating state information systems, information systems of [government agencies](https://tadviser.com/index.php/State), local authorities and subordinate organizations. The project involves the creation and development of information systems on the Gostech website.

The Chairman of the Board [Russian Bar Association](https://tadviser.com/index.php?title=Company:Russian_Bar_Association&action=edit&redlink=1) [Vladimir Gruzdev](https://tadviser.com/index.php?title=Person:Vladimir_Gruzdev&action=edit&redlink=1) noted that the platform will allow federal and regional authorities to create state information systems and digital services. The platform will allow some departments to use the technical developments of others, so the tasks will not be duplicated, and the costs [IT](https://tadviser.com/index.php/IT) will be less.

So far, the work of the platform is regulated by presidential decree No. 231 of 2023 and the position of the government No. 2338 of 2022. The bill will replace this decree and provision. Deputy Prime Minister - Head of [the Government](https://tadviser.com/index.php?title=Person:Dmitry_Yuryevich_Grigorenko&action=edit&redlink=1) Apparatus [Dmitry Grigorenko](https://tadviser.com/index.php?title=Person:Dmitry_Yuryevich_Grigorenko&action=edit&redlink=1) said that the draft law expands the composition of participants and delimits areas of responsibility between them.

The document provides for the integration of non-commercial and commercial participants. For commercial participants, a paid use of the site will be provided. This will allow the platform to receive additional funding from business users.

The representative of Ministry of Digital Development said that the bill will speed up the transition to the platform of all authorities and organizations, as well as simplify the creation of state information systems. The bill will fix the processes of their operation. This will ensure that approaches to building and supporting digital systems are consistent.[\[6\]](https://tadviser.com#cite_note-5)

#### SaaS and a single cluster of AI services. The road map "GosTech 2.0" is presented

In mid-October 2025, the roadmap of the unified digital platform "GosTech 2.0" was published - a platform for the development and operation of state information systems (GIS). Further development of the project is part of large-scale changes in the management approach [state](https://tadviser.com/index.php/State) [IT infrastructure](https://tadviser.com/index.php/IT_infrastructure) implemented by the Government of the Russian Federation.

As noted, the key features of GosTech 2.0 will be the complete automation of GIS ordering procedures, as well as the creation of a competitive environment by including [IT solutions](https://tadviser.com/index.php/IT) from various Russian developers in the platform. GosTech 2.0 will make it faster and easier to create new services and ensure a high level of their security. The main goal is to make government digital solutions as efficient and convenient as possible for citizens and businesses.

In accordance with the roadmap, in January 2026, within the framework of the GosTech 2.0 project, a public cloud will be announced and SaaS ([software](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Software) as a service) services will be launched in pilot mode. After about two months, in March, it is planned to audit the IT infrastructure of the Federal Investment [Fund](https://tadviser.com/index.php/IT_infrastructure) and the constituent entities of the Russian Federation, to form a list of GIS and standard solutions for implementation on a new platform. The completion of the creation of GosTech 2.0 with the formation of the necessary infrastructure is scheduled for April 2026. In November 2026, a single cluster of AI services will be launched within the framework of the platform. In another month, additional information security requirements and redundancy of the computing infrastructure are expected to be implemented. And in December 2027, systems deployed on the basis of GosTech 1.0 will be migrated to the new platform.

The new IT management model provides for a clear separation of responsibilities in the development of government services, platforms and other digital projects. In particular, all state IT solutions are planned to be developed with the support of the Center for Expertise and Coordination of Informatization.[\[7\]](https://tadviser.com#cite_note-6)

#### Prime Minister Mikhail Mishustin: 52 federal and regional systems work at Gostekh, another 30 services are in development

There are 52 federal and regional systems operating at [GosTech](https://tadviser.com/index.php/State_that), another 30 services are in development or pilot operation. This was announced on September 23, 2025 by Prime Minister [Mikhail Mishustin](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Person:Mishustin_Mikhail_Vladimirovich), speaking at a strategic session on the development of a single digital platform "GosTech."

As the Prime Minister emphasized, on the basis of the Gostech platform, significant services are being successfully implemented in key social areas, such as [health](https://tadviser.com/index.php?title=Pharmaceuticals,_Medicine,_Healthcare&action=edit&redlink=1) care and education. As an example, she cited the mobile application "My School" launched by the Ministry of [Education](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Ministry_of_Education). This service, as Mikhail Mishustin explained, provides students and their parents with a convenient tool for tracking the schedule of lessons, homework and academic performance, and also allows you to form a single child schedule, including visiting circles and sections, and provides access to an extensive library of electronic educational materials.

In parallel, according to the Prime Minister, regional services in the areas of social protection, construction and housing supervision operate in pilot mode on the Gostech platform. #### How to assess the economic and technological feasibility of creating state information systems on the Gostech platform. A manual has been published

The general order of evaluation, in particular, includes sequential steps:

- determination of the level of technological maturity of GIS and the presence of technological risks;
- determining the economic efficiency of the Gostech platform;
- assessment of GIS significance;
- determining the priority of measures for the creation, development and operation of GIS on the Gostech platform;
- making a decision on the feasibility of creating or developing GIS on the Gostech platform;
- inclusion of the system in the plan for creation and development of systems on the Gostech platform.


The largest volume of the document falls on recommendations for assessing technological maturity, including such criteria as:

- the scope of measures taken to protect the information contained in the GIS;
- the degree of dependence of GIS operation on the use of foreign programs for electronic computers and databases, IT and hardware;
- reliability of GIS operation and their availability to users;
- provision of GIS with infrastructure necessary for their operation;
- scalability;
- clientocentricity;
-  maturity of GIS architecture and [software](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Software) process components.


To assess the economic feasibility, the methodological recommendations provide, among other things, a formula for calculating the total costs of creating, developing and operating GIS on the Gostech platform, a methodology for calculating the costs of operating measures, and the costs of the authority to use commercial additional platform services.

##### Prerequisites for the creation of the Gostech platform

According to Deputy Prime Minister [of Russia](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Russia) Dmitry Chernyshenko, the state spends huge amounts of money on IT. The total cost of owning regional and federal information systems is 460 billion [rubles](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Article:Russian_ruble) per year. Half of this amount is federal budget expenditures, half - regions. Departmental programs of digital transformation of federal executive bodies (FOIV) for the development of information systems laid 170 billion rubles for three years. Moreover, this amount implies only the development of [software](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Software) and does not include hardware costs.

As Dmitry Chernyshenko noted, 80% of the functionality of the existing GIS coincides, but there is no possibility of reusing it. In addition, developers are expensive. Moreover, the country is sorely short of qualified personnel, the deficit is about one million specialists. Therefore, today it is simply impossible to work as before, when each FEI created information systems independently and built its own [data center](https://tadviser.com/index.php/DPC).

The head of the Ministry of Digital Development, Communications and Mass Media (Mintsifra) Maksut Shadayev also believes that it [is](<https://tadviser.com/index.php/Project:Ministry_of_Digital_Development,_Communications_and_Mass_Media_of_the_Russian_Federation_(Ministry_of_Digital_Development)>) impossible to design and create systems as it was done before. Now there is no time to write the terms of reference for six months, none of the heads of departments is ready to put up with such deadlines. Moreover, the existing IT paradigms make it possible to do everything much faster by using and reusing ready-made software components.

##### The tasks that Gostech solves

According to Maksut Shadayev, the main task of introducing the platform is to provide the best technologies on the market according to the model "you pay only for what you use."

|  | "Gostech" will allow us to move to the model, when the customer will pay only for what he uses. Within the framework of the platform, we will offer the market the best existing technologies that will be in demand by departments. The developed service should be available to other customers in the shortest possible time, taking into account security. Regional departments that do not have the necessary resources will be able to connect to the platform and use ready-made services. Given the increase in the overall level of digital literacy in government agencies, additional settings will also be able to be made by department experts using the Low Code/No Code platforms, "Maksut Shadayev said. |  | 

In general, the head of the department responsible for the development of the Gostech platform identified five basic tasks:

- attract advanced technologies to the public sector;
- implement the best development experience;
- ensure the rapid launch of state and regional services;
- create a unified technological environment and a safe infrastructure for the development of state services;
- Implement uniform development management standards.


##### Financial incentives to fill the Gostech platform

As Maksut Shadayev said, it is planned to distribute 600 million [rubles](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Article:Russian_ruble) within 3 years to support the creation of regional services using the Gostech tools. Moreover, 300 million rubles have already been pledged for 2022. According to the minister, in order to support the regions, it will be possible to attract even a larger amount than planned. In addition, today FOIVs submit requests for additional funding for the development of state information systems, priority will be given to those who intend to use the Gostech tools for development.

Private developers are supposed to be interested in the ability to implement their services through the Gostech platform without going through the public procurement procedure. According to Maksut Shadayev, the platform is open, and each developer can create services on it or even transfer existing developments, fulfilling all existing requirements for embedding into the platform.

##### Regulatory regulation

According to Maksut Shadayev, the issue of transferring the development of all state and regional services to the Gostech platform from 2024 is being discussed, so all federal departments and regions need to prepare for this.

Director of the PKU "Gostekh" Vasily Slushkin said that today it takes 1.5-2 years to put the system into operation with a full life cycle, since the only way to start the system is a cascade life cycle. To change the situation, it is necessary to amend the Decree of the Government of the Russian Federation of 06.07.2015 No. 676 "On the requirements for the procedure for the creation, development, commissioning, operation and decommissioning of state information systems and further storage of information contained in their databases." If you legally allow the use of an iterative life cycle, then the GIS launch speed will increase several times. And the time from the start of development to the first launch will be reduced to 3-6 months.

To ensure the reuse of software components, it is necessary to amend the Resolution of January 30, 2013 No. 62 "On the National Fund of Algorithms and Programs for Electronic Computers." The concept of a repository of programs that can be reused and the concept of an open license should be introduced. The corresponding document has already passed all the approval procedures and is in the government apparatus. It can be accepted until the end of October 2021.

##### How to get on the platform

According to Vasily Slushkin, access to the platform can be obtained in several ways. Thus, authorities and institutions can apply through [the Ministry of Digital Development](<https://tadviser.com/index.php/Project:Ministry_of_Digital_Development,_Communications_and_Mass_Media_of_the_Russian_Federation_(Ministry_of_Digital_Development)>) and, in accordance with the Decree of the Government of the Russian Federation of 12.10.2020 No. 1674 "On Conducting an Experiment on the Creation, Translation and Development of State Information Systems and Their Components on the Unified Digital Platform of the [Russian Federation](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Russia)" Gosteh "to be included in the experiment. After the completion of the experiment, from May 31, 2022, the application can be submitted through the Gostech portal.

The director of PKU Gostech recalls that in September 2021, new provisions were adopted on the inclusion of new components. Now, if the [state authority](https://tadviser.com/index.php?title=State_and_social_structures&action=edit&redlink=1) wants to contribute a particular product to the Gostech platform, then after checking quality and safety control, consideration at the architectural council and the presidium of the legal commission, the decision can be made to the platform.

If [the software](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Software) belongs to a private company, then it can also get to the platform after passing the established bureaucratic procedures.

##### Development without programming and "Gosmarket"

First Deputy Director of Gostech PKU [Vladimir Rakhmanov](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Person:Vladimir_Viktorovich_Rakhmanov) added that products on the Gostech platform should support the Low-code/No-code concept. That is, the creation of new services will not require programming. In his opinion, the level of experts in the departments is high enough for them to be able to build services from ready-made modules. This approach will make it possible to reuse the components as much as possible and solve the problem of a shortage of qualified specialists.

According to Vladimir Rakhmanov, state service customers, forming a technical task for developers, will understand which modules are unique and which are typical, and will indicate this.

All applications of the Gostech platform will be available on the [Gosmarket](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Article:State_market_-_state_marketplace_of_IT_services_and_applications) technology marketplace. On this site, government service developers will be able to purchase ready-made modules and standard products so as not to waste time creating them, but to focus on writing a unique part of the systems, which will save time and improve the quality of development.

By 2024, the share of unique development should be significantly reduced in favor of applications from the State Market.

##### Migration of services to the Gostech platform

According to [Ivan Operchuk](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Person:Operchuk_Ivan), deputy director of the Gostech PKU, there are more than 4.1 thousand information systems in [Russia](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Russia), more than 3.3 thousand of them are regional. Today the task is to transfer these systems and services to the Gostech platform. However, migration requires reengineering systems, it does not make sense to transfer systems and services as they exist now. Migration of systems should bring real improvements to the lives of citizens and businesses. In addition, the platform and system should be used as efficiently as possible after the migration. Based on these principles, Gostech PKU has developed principles for transferring client and support systems.

Client systems can migrate to the platform in two ways: as part of the design of the client path of the domain; within the framework of ranking according to the method (by the degree of maturity of the system and the mass of requests).

Criteria for assessing the maturity of client systems:

- online by default;
- feedback;
- online payment;
- proactivity;
- time of filling in the forms is not more than 10 minutes;
- Selecting a notification channel
- One-time data entry
- status tracking;
- Quick search for services/services
- use on mobile devices;
- iterative form filling;
- accessibility for persons with disabilities.


Support systems are evaluated by the level of digital maturity and planned load. So, it makes no sense to transfer a small regional system to the platform if it can be replaced with a typical solution that will be used not in one, but in many regions at once. Moreover, after the reengineering of such a system, Gostech is likely to transfer other small systems closely related to it, the migration of which would not make sense under other conditions.

Criteria for assessing the maturity of support systems:

- Reusable
-  Binding to [a software](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Software) vendor
- Binding to equipment supplier
- microservices;
-  availability of business logic in [DBMS](https://tadviser.com/index.php/DBMS);
- containerization;
- Automatic scaling
- pilot zones;
- Smooth replication of changes
- load quota arrangement;
- horizontal scaling.


Stages of organization of transition to Gostech:

- August 2021 - Ranking method developed and tested;
- September 2021 - Feedback session on the methodology was held;
- October 2021 - Revision and publication of the methodology based on the results of testing;
- November 2021 - FGIS CI: data collection form according to the method;
- December 2021 - The FGIS CI has made assessments on information systems;
- April 2022 - Ranking and formation of a plan for the transition of regional systems to Gostech.


##### Domain approach to launching services on the Gostech platform

Ivan Operchuk from PKU Gostech says that the Gostech platform is built in accordance with the domain principle. Domains are the sphere of activity of the [state](https://tadviser.com/index.php/State) belonging to one subject area. Domain architecture is consistent with enterprise architecture and consists of business architecture, application architecture, information architecture, and technical architecture. When designing a domain, the needs of the client and all his life situations must be taken into account. This approach considers all the client's activities within the subject area at all levels - federal, regional, municipal.

The domain's area of   activity is very extensive and contains hundreds or even thousands of client paths. Therefore, you need to approach the design of client paths and services iteratively. Implementing first the most popular, and then returning and creating the next priority client paths and services.

The first domains on Gostech:

[health care](https://tadviser.com/index.php?title=Pharmaceuticals,_Medicine,_Healthcare&action=edit&redlink=1);
[science](https://tadviser.com/index.php?title=Education_and_Science&action=edit&redlink=1) and education;
public transport;
construction and housing and [communal services](https://tadviser.com/index.php?title=Housing_and_communal_services,_service_and_household_services&action=edit&redlink=1);
public administration.

##### Experience with domain approach in healthcare

Deputy Minister of [Health](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Health_care) of the Russian Federation Pavel Pugachev spoke about the approaches to creating the Healthcare domain. It is important that health care is human-centered, and doctors get rid of unnecessary bureaucracy, spending more time on patients. To improve the quality of medical services, an effective domain structure is needed. Today, the main data array is at the regional level, with medical organizations, while the information is very poorly used. As a result, so-called industry wells are formed. The transition from industry wells to a domain structure will make it possible to de-publish master data, forming a single base for all industry services and departments.

According to the representative of the Ministry of Health, as part of the pilot project for the creation of the Healthcare domain, a group of more than 100 experts analyzed client paths for 6 weeks. As a result, the top 10 directions were allocated for priority actions taking into account the needs of patients and the industry. The business architecture has been identified, more than 40 services with high replication potential in the regions have been identified. Based on the requirements of the business architecture, the technological architecture is designed. The development was carried out from the needs of man. The ultimate goal of the entire transformation of the industry is to move to personalized [medicine](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Medicine), providing assistance exactly to the extent that each specific person needs it.

According to Pavel Pugachev, it is planned to add a platform on top of the existing layer of legacy systems, which includes a digital profile of a doctor, a digital profile of a patient and a digital profile of a medical organization. The transition from master data to a digital profile will allow you to receive information about the current state of a medical organization, doctor or patient in real time. This will provide an opportunity to model processes and start using recommendation medical systems. Which in turn will reduce the number of medical errors and increase the quality of medical services. By 2030, legacy systems will be gone, and everything will switch to a single platform principle.

#### The Ministry of Digital Development is creating an interdepartmental working group on GosTech

 [The Ministry of Digital Development](<https://tadviser.com/index.php/Project:Ministry_of_Digital_Development,_Communications_and_Mass_Media_of_the_Russian_Federation_(Ministry_of_Digital_Development)>) decided to create an interdepartmental working group to coordinate the activities of the participants in the experiment to create and develop state information systems and their components on a single digital platform "GosTech." The draft of this order was published on July 21, 2021 on the official website for posting draft regulatory legal acts<sup>[\[51\]](https://tadviser.com#cite_note-50)</sup> of<sup>[\[52\]](https://tadviser.com#cite_note-51)</sup>.

Among the tasks of the working group are to consider issues and develop proposals for the creation, translation and development of state information systems and their components on the GosTech platform, and develop criteria for assessing the results of the experiment.

The tasks also include considering issues and preparing proposals for amending the legislation of the Russian Federation, necessary for the creation and development of state information systems and their components on the GosTech platform.

As conceived by the Ministry of Digital Development, the working group will submit reports to the government of the Russian Federation, including, among other things, proposals for the development and refinement of GosTech, proposals for the target architecture and data model of this platform.

The working group will include representatives of the Federal Investment Fund, state extrabudgetary funds, the autonomous non-profit organization [Analytical Center under the Government of the Russian Federation](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Company:Analytical_Center_under_the_Government_of_the_Russian_Federation), the [Russian Export Center](<https://tadviser.com/index.php/Company:Russian_Export_Center_(REC)>) and other organizations operating in the field of IT by agreement. The structure of the working group should be approved by the order of the Ministry of Digital Development.

Meetings of the working group will be held at least once a month.
