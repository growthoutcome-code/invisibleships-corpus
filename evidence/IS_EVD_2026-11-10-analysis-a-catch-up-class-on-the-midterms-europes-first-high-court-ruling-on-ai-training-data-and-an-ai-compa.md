## Captured text

It’s been a few weeks since our last bonus class, as I’ve been on the road in October. So it’s catch-up time with more bonus classes coming! (Including a deeper dive on the spy saga at Nvidia and how that relates to our class on US export controls).

Today, this is a catch up for those of you wondering about the impact of the midterms on AI governance, what happened on Election Day in Europe and their first high-court opinion on AI training data and copyright (and whether they can mine text without running afoul of copyright laws), and how an AI company’s bankruptcy proceedings connect up to the conversations we’ve been having. So, a quick video address from me on these issues, a link to all of your class cards so you can catch up (or join us on the leaderboard), and links to the three readings this bonus class is based on.

And I heard your preference! You want the lectures written up here to pace yourself through, so they’re coming (albeit a little slowly — you guys seem like you need some time to catch up on past classes, and I need to some time to write these up!).

**This post is bonus content that goes “beyond” what we covered in the live class. Bonus classes include a video address from me, and are designed paid subscribers who want to go deeper into AI Law and Policy, who want to support my work (thank you!), have access to the class readings, video assignments, or reach me via virtual chat-based office-hours.**
