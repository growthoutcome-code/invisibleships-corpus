## Captured text

Karnataka, the Indian state, has ordered AI face-scan attendance for government and aided schools starting in the 2026-27 school year, [according to Careers360's June report](https://news.careers360.com/karnataka-launches-nirantara-ai-based-facial-recognition-attendance-system-for-schools-pu-colleges-students-government/amp). A new global map from Privacy International (PI) shows how often such systems are bought, mandated or quietly installed before families get a say. The London-based digital rights charity's [explainer, dated Monday, Nov. 9](https://privacyinternational.org/explainer/5885/mapping-facial-recognition-schools), logs named schools, vendors, incidents and regulatory actions, and says no systematic global record of the technology's spread into classrooms existed before it.

For parents, teachers and anyone who funds public schools, the stake is concrete: a child's face becomes a stored identifier. In Karnataka, officials have said attendance data would also [help identify actual meal beneficiaries](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fkarnataka%2Fai-based-facial-recognition-tech-for-govt-school-attendance-3462631) for mid-day meals and eggs.

### Karnataka Makes Face-Scan Attendance Compulsory in State-Run Schools

A government order assigns the Centre for e-Governance to implement the "Nirantara" system in government and aided schools and pre-university colleges, at about Rs 2.16 crore (roughly $227,000), [according to Careers360 reporting](https://news.careers360.com/karnataka-launches-nirantara-ai-based-facial-recognition-attendance-system-for-schools-pu-colleges-students-government/amp). The outlet reports that e-attendance becomes compulsory for students in government and aided primary schools, high schools and pre-university colleges in 2026-27.

The earlier plan covered [52,686 schools and 52 lakh students](https://news.careers360.com/karnataka-scrap-facial-recognition-attendance-govt-school-data-privacy-mid-day-meal-ai-app-digital-madhu-bangarappa-education-news/amp): 46,460 government schools, 6,226 aided schools and more than 5.2 million students. The path has not been straight. In June 2025, the education department said it had no plan for a statewide rollout, days after Education Minister Madhu Bangarappa told the state assembly the technology would be used. The scheme returned a year later with a government order.

### How Does Face-Scan Attendance Work, and Where Does the Face Data Go?

A 2025 directive described AI-powered e-attendance linked to the state's existing student-tracking app, SATS. The system converts face images into encrypted IDs "designed to ensure data privacy and prevent reverse engineering," with data hosted at the Karnataka State Data Centre. Officials said teachers would [photograph classrooms with a phone app](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fkarnataka%2Fai-based-facial-recognition-tech-for-govt-school-attendance-3462631), and that photographs of children would not be stored.

The engineering distinction matters. Matching a face to one enrolled record, a one-to-one check, differs from searching a crowd for any face on a list, a one-to-many search. Attendance systems are typically the first kind, but a stored face template remains a biometric identifier that cannot be reset if leaked. The state's encrypted-ID description is an official claim; the sources reviewed contain no independent audit of the system.

### Officials Cite Meal-Scheme Accuracy; Opponents Cite Leaks and Failed Scans

Officials said the system would produce accurate attendance, flag irregular students and determine the actual beneficiaries of the mid-day meal, Ksheera Bhagya milk and egg schemes. One official said it would make it easy to compare actual attendance with enrollment. PI's reading is that "a child's lunch becomes conditional on a face scan." The June 2026 order coverage reviewed does not itself mention a meal linkage, so that link rests on the 2025 plan and PI's account.

A coalition of 31 educationists, teachers' unions and parents' groups [wrote to the chief minister](https://www.inkl.com/news/classrooms-to-be-fitted-with-ai-face-recognition-to-track-student-attendance-in-india) warning that children's facial data could be misused if leaked or stolen. Spokesperson Niranjanaradhya VP said schools "must remain safe spaces, not zones of surveillance." Child rights advocate Nagasimha G Rao said the government must explain why the technology is needed and how data will be protected, and parents worried that students could be wrongly marked absent and lose benefits if scans fail. Telangana's chief minister, A. Revanth Reddy, [directed officials in August 2025](https://news.careers360.com/telangana-cm-orders-infrastructure-upgrades-mandates-facial-recognition-in-schools-colleges) to introduce mandated facial recognition for students and teaching staff, according to the Press Trust of India.

### Serbian Procurement Records Show Face-Capable Equipment Institutions Say They Do Not Use

PI says investigators found face-recognition-capable cameras in schools only by digging through procurement data. Reporters from the Balkan Investigative Reporting Network (BIRN) and partner outlets [located more than 30 purchases](https://en.nuns.rs/?p=7011) with facial-recognition-related specifications between 2022 and the end of 2024, using 32 access-to-information requests, to which 22 institutions fully responded and 10 did not. Many records show equipment [bought with facial recognition support](https://www.vreme.com/en/drustvo/birn-pazi-mreza-masovnog-nadzora-snimaju-te-biometrijske-kamere/) even where institutions say they do not use that function, most purchases show no trace of data protection impact assessments, and some institutions do not admit to having biometric systems. That gap between what is purchased and what is disclosed is the transparency problem PI's map is meant to expose.

### Texas, Michigan and New York Show Three Different US Outcomes

Texas' Barbers Hill school district appears in PI's catalogue with [AnyVision software at 32 entryways](https://privacyinternational.org/examples/5839/barbers-hill-school-district-uses-facial-recognition-technology), a $131,000 system with a watch list covering students, staff and "people of interest," including suspended and expelled students. That entry is dated Oct. 30, 2019, and PI's launch material does not say whether the system is still running. The same vendor's tool drew scrutiny in Santa Fe, Texas, where [records obtained by The Markup](https://themarkup.org/privacy/2021/07/06/this-manual-for-a-popular-facial-recognition-tool-shows-just-how-much-the-software-tracks-people) showed a pilot logging more than 164,000 detections in seven days and a vendor email saying it "detected one student 1100 times." Detections are not confirmed identifications, and the district did not comment.

In Detroit, Visitor Aware, from Singlewire Software, screens visitors at school kiosks against the National Sex Offender Registry and FBI databases; Singlewire says it collects no facial data from students. Molly Kleinman of the University of Michigan's Ford School [told Michigan Public](https://www.michiganpublic.org/transportation-infrastructure/2026-04-28/facial-recognition-is-now-in-some-michigan-schools-but-critics-are-concerned) the technology is mostly trained on white adult male faces, performs worse on people of color, and that "you only have one face."

New York sits at the other end. Education Commissioner Betty Rosa [ordered a statewide school ban](https://www.asumag.com/safety-security/article/21274626/new-york-state-bans-use-of-facial-recognition-technology-in-schools) on purchasing or using facial recognition in September 2023, after a state technology office analysis said the risks may outweigh the benefits, citing potentially higher false-positive rates for people of color, women and children. The order followed Lockport, where a district [stopped using a $1.4 million system](https://www.audacy.com/wben/news/local/lockport-superintendent-addresses-states-ban-on-facial-recognition-software-in-schools) that was decommissioned in 2020. Rosa's order leaves digital fingerprinting and other biometrics to local districts.

### Sweden and Britain Penalized Schools While India's Data Law Phases In

Sweden's data protection authority [fined Skellefteå municipality 200,000 kronor](https://privacyinternational.org/examples/5830/swedish-school-fined-facial-recognition-use) (roughly $20,000) in 2019 over a trial that used facial recognition to track attendance for 22 high-school students over three weeks, finding that parental consent was not a valid basis and that less intrusive options existed. In the United Kingdom, the Information Commissioner's Office [reprimanded Chelmer Valley High School](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/23/uk-school-reprimanded-for-unlawful-use-of-facial-recognition-technology) in July 2024 for using facial recognition for lunch payments from March 2023 without a prior data protection impact assessment and with an opt-out consent process.

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, bars tracking and behavioral monitoring of children and processing detrimental to their well-being, but provisions take effect in stages, and [children's-data duties start May 2027](https://legal500.com/intelligence/india/privacy/update-%E2%80%93-india's-digital-personal-data-protection-framework-comes-into-effect). Whether and when those child protections reach a state attendance system is not settled in the sources reviewed.

### UN Expert Wants Facial Recognition Banned in All Educational Settings

PI says the UN Special Rapporteur on the right to education [recommended a ban in November 2024](https://privacyinternational.org/news-analysis/5461/un-expert-recommends-ban-facial-recognition-schools) that would bar facial recognition from educational institutions. PI's submission to the Rapporteur's 2025 report on the right to be safe in education (A/HRC/59/41) [quotes the demand that](https://privacyinternational.org/advocacy/5664/pis-submission-right-be-safe-education) "facial recognition must be banned in all educational settings."

PI also cites reports of use in [nearly 1,000 schools in China](https://privacyinternational.org/examples/facial-recognition) and more than 60 institutions in Russia, though the Russian figure traces to [a Nov. 23, 2021 VisionLabs announcement](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202111/visionlabs-biometrics-deployed-at-more-than-60-universities-schools-in-russia) that covers universities as well as schools. Treat these as indicators, not a census.

### Map Entries Record Reports, Not Current Operation, and PI Wants Schools Banned

PI argues children generally cannot consent, that schools' power over pupils undermines real choice, that systems risk bias and often lack data protection safeguards, and that constant monitoring erodes trust. PI is an advocacy group with a stated position that the technology should be banned in schools, and many map examples span several years, so a listing records a reported deployment rather than a system running today. Karnataka's government and districts that adopted systems after school shootings argue they improve accuracy and security. PI accepts public submissions of examples at pvcy.org/frtmap.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is facial recognition legal in schools?

It depends on the jurisdiction. New York State bars it outright, Sweden fined a municipality under EU data rules, and the UK's regulator reprimanded a school over its lunch-payment system, while Karnataka has ordered it for state-run schools.

### Can a failed face scan cost a child a meal in Karnataka?

Parents have said they fear students could be wrongly marked absent and lose benefits if scans fail, and officials have said the system would help determine actual meal-scheme beneficiaries; the sources reviewed do not describe an appeals process.

### What is the difference between checking attendance and scanning a crowd?

Attendance matching compares a face to one enrolled record, while crowd or watch-list systems such as the one catalogued in Barbers Hill search many faces against a list that can include "people of interest."

### How can I find out whether my child's school uses it?

PI's map is a starting point, but its listings record reports rather than current operation, so ask the school or district in writing what system is installed and whether a data protection impact assessment was done; PI also takes submissions at pvcy.org/frtmap.
