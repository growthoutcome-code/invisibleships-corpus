## Captured text

Two months after opening its laboratory in Palo Alto, CA, neurotech start-up Subsense has started the first proof-of-concept studies of its nonsurgical, nanoparticle-based approach. By the end of 2026, the company hopes to demonstrate through mouse studies that nanoparticle technology may be able to treat symptoms of Parkinson’s disease, epilepsy or traumatic brain injury, Subsense founder and CEO Tetiana Aleksandrova told *Medtech Insight*.
