## Captured text

A Kentucky police officer sued for wrongly arresting and jailing a man for more than two weeks because artificial intelligence misidentified him as the perpetrator of a crime failed to convince a federal court to dismiss the civil rights case.

The Fourth Amendment doesn’t allow a law enforcement official to delegate the task of assessing probable cause to an AI tool, or to arrest someone based on the AI tool’s uncorroborated conclusion that the suspect has committed a crime, Judge S. Chad Meredith of the US District Court for the Eastern District of Kentucky [said](https://www.bloomberglaw.com/public/document/OsoriovKilmerDocketNo226cv00004EDKyJan082026CourtDocket?doc_id=X1BJNEQRTC38UBO6EVQMNNS9235) Monday.
