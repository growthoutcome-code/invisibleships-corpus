### 1. State Level:

The Colorado Attorney General

As of August 7, 2024, the Colorado Privacy Act (CPA) was expanded by HB24-1058. This law classifies "neural data" (information generated by the measurement of the brain or nervous system) as "sensitive data."

  

**Role:** The Attorney General’s office is the primary enforcement body for these privacy protections.

Accountability: If neuro-technology is being used to collect, process, or manipulate neural data without explicit "opt-in" consent, it is a violation of state law.

  

**Action:** You would file a complaint with the Colorado Attorney General's Consumer Protection division.
