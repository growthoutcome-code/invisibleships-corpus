# The law: European Union

## The AI Act, as amended in 2026

- **Status:** in force since 1 August 2024 and applied in stages. Its bans on certain AI practices have applied since 2 February 2025. Most transparency duties apply from 2 August 2026.
- **The 2026 amendment:** Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, published 24 July 2026 and in force from 27 July 2026, moved the duties for high-risk AI. They now apply from **2 December 2027** for stand-alone systems such as those used in hiring, credit and education, and from **2 August 2028** for AI built into regulated products such as medical devices. It also added bans on AI that generates child sexual abuse material and on "nudifier" apps.
- **What it covers:** banned practices; heavy duties for high-risk systems; transparency for chatbots and synthetic media; rules for general-purpose AI models, supervised by the EU's AI Office.
- **What it means for a person there:** some AI uses on people are banned outright. Many protections for high-risk uses now start in late 2027 or 2028.
- **What it does not cover:** AI used only for military, defence or national security; it does not create a general right to mental privacy.
- **Analysis:** Nita Farahany ([*Thinking Freely*, 12 November 2025](https://nitafarahany.substack.com/p/understanding-how-the-eu-regulates)) describes one AI system falling under six or more EU laws at once, including the GDPR, the Data Act, the Digital Services Act and cybersecurity rules, with overlapping impact assessments and incident reports due to different authorities on different deadlines.
- **Sources:** [Regulation (EU) 2024/1689](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj) and [Regulation (EU) 2026/1744](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/1744/oj) (EUR-Lex); White & Case, ["EU AI Omnibus enters into force"](https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/eu-ai-omnibus-enters-force-amending-ai-act) (July 2026).
