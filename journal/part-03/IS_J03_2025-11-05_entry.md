My individual suppression and the threat of the so-called euthanization “ugly workflows” is still present at the intersection of 8th Ave. & Colorado Blvd. in Denver, Colorado. People continue to be brutalized with an un-consented “Citizen Tech” conversation including the suggestion “Do not support this person with the sign!” or “Do not support this person to the left of you!”. The “Citizen Tech” suggestion seems to emanate from the vehicle itself and if the vehicle responds, they are added to the “Euthanization List”.

The phenomena of sound at the intersection suggests that if you are within my presence and you are thinking or reading my sign for example, your thoughts are completely transparent and they can be heard at the intersection, by myself or anyone passing by. I often recommend in “Citizen Tech” conversation that people hold their ears shut with their index fingers to test for a change in the volume of the conversation. If there is an increase in the volume of the “Citizen Tech” conversation, your ears are in use. Strangely, that is both the suggestion and the human experience for myself and at least one other brave human being that approached me at the DRM (Denver Rescue Mission) 48th St. Shelter asking me about the strange conversation in the building and using the word “acoustics”.

Historically, 30-50 vehicles per week conservatively speaking are added to the euthanization list from the same location. Imagine a six lane thoroughfare. Three lanes on each side. I’m standing in the middle on the median facing the southbound drivers side window with vehicles backing up for several hundred feet at each stop light. Anytime I look in the direction of a vehicle the unsolicited “Citizen Tech” communication begins “Do not support this person, to the left of you!”. Unfortunately there is no way of avoiding looking at the people driving by and for every three to four stops a “Citizen Tech” comment would emanate from a specific vehicle. For example, “Why are you saying this to me?” or “Don’t worry. I wont.”.
