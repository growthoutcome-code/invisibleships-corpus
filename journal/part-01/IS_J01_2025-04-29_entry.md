**date uncertain over several hours, Cirrus Building, possible law enforcement involvement and a murder -** 

**7pm-midnight est.:** Suggestions of a woman directly above me was having an emotional breakdown after being confronted by 1-2 people (paraphrasing) “He’s really not a terrorist? Are you sure?” **Female, crying, distraught* followed immediately by (paraphrasing) “No he’s really not a terrorist. He’s a good person and we need you to pack your things and leave the building.” **Male, supportive* continuing on… (paraphrasing) “I’m afraid to leave. Am I going to prison?” **Female, crying, distraught *Partially audible?, Received telepathically unless otherwise noted*

In retrospect: This confrontation felt calm and contained for the most part. My assumption was that this was the intelligence community or the military attempting to remove the threat from the building. Was this another mock arrest or an actual intervention? Her comments indicated a person possibly suffering from a fixed belief system fueled by religious ideals, me being a threat to the community and or suffering from her exposure to an unacknowledged telepathic phenomena. 

**Midnight-11am est:** I woke from my sleep in the late night or early morning hours to a handful of telepathic suggestions both verbal and visual. The suggestions included a female euthanizing a male with a syringe in the apartment directly above me who had participated in the orchestration of the tormenting telepathic communication I have been experiencing. Suggestions included the language  (paraphrasing) “You made a mistake. You just couldn’t stop recruiting people could you?” **Female, calm, Character D?, Deborah H.?*  A few hours later someone being dissolved in a bathtub to hide the evidence was suggested followed later with  investigators arriving later to an unclean bathroom. The victim was later suggested to be (paraphrasing statement) “Scot.., Scott Thompson” **Female voice, calm, concerned* **All suggestions received telepathically unless otherwise noted*

  

**In retrospect:** This is a challenging situation because these could be thoughts in advance of an event or they could be the events themselves. In addition, if I am experiencing technologically manufactured telepathy these suggestions and or events may have taken place in another physical location away from the Cirrus building, thus falsely implicating the people above me. 

What brought an eerie suggestion of reality was how the hours later in the morning felt, emotionally speaking. Normally the emotional sensations I experience in the apartment day-to-day feel like a thick and inflammatory blanket of silence waiting to be punctured with the sharp verbal suggestion of a “euthanization” needle. In contrast, later that morning the emotional sensation was just as unusual as the telepathic silence that came with it. The completely silent apartment felt like it was submerged in luke-warm water, emotionless with the exception of the heaviness of a from possibly someone else’s indifference, tearful regret swimming with an odd sense of duty.

As I’m finally and reluctantly entering this information into the journal I’m wondering if I will ever find the luxurious time and space it would take to process this nightmare in its entirety. All things considered, I’m emotionally and physically healthy and moving forward in life. However, I carry a great deal of concern with me for the other people on both sides of this nightmare and that concern grows daily, especially if it is unexpectedly affecting our first responders, intelligence community and the military. Stay in school everyone. Keep working on yourselves and avoid talking to strangers.
