**Thursday 02/27/25 between 3-5pm near St Mark's Coffee Shop in Denver, CO** Middle eastern male voice claims responsibility for a Terrorist attack - 

“This is a terrorist attack on America” 

**Received telepathically from what sounded like a voice from a middle eastern male.* 

Someone claimed to be from the Saudi Royal Family and that they were responsible for a terrorist attack on the United States of America. At their request, I repeated telepathically the statement cited above along with other statements making sure that I included information in the telepathic communication to separate myself from the statement i.e. 

“This is the voice of Sean Christopher Harris and I am being asked to repeat a statement…”. 

Additional information was suggested telepathically that the CIA is also attempting to claim responsibility for telepathic phenomena of communication 

(paraphrasing) “We absolutely need control of this method of communication”.**Received telepathically*

Other statements from the middle eastern male voice include

(paraphrasing) “America did this to us and now we are doing this to you America” *Received telepathically* (paraphrasing)

##### **Questions & Comments:**

  - How do we know the person making this statement is from the Saudi Royal family? I don't. Nothing in this exchange identified the speaker, and nothing since has. An identity claimed inside an anonymous communication is the easiest thing in the world to say and the hardest thing to check.
  - Historically, what is presented to citizens verbally and visually may involve disguise — I have experienced suggestions of a Caucasian woman presenting as a Middle Eastern man. If a voice can be costumed, then "sounded like a middle eastern male" identifies nobody, and that description is the only one I had here.
  - Claiming responsibility is not the same as being responsible. I am not aware of any attack on the United States on or around 02/27/25 that has been publicly connected to this statement, and I make no claim that one occurred.
  - At their request I repeated the statement telepathically, and while doing it I made a point of saying "This is the voice of Sean Christopher Harris and I am being asked to repeat a statement…". I did that unprompted on the first day. I would ask anyone reading this to notice why I felt I needed to.
  - "America did this to us and now we are doing this to you America" — I have no way to know whether that is a real grievance, a script, or someone repeating what they were told to say. All three are consistent with what I heard.
  - So the question, and it is a question: is this evidence of retaliation against the United States? In August 2025 a joint investigation by The Guardian, [+972 Magazine](https://www.972mag.com/microsoft-cloud-israel-8200-expose/) and Local Call reported that an Israeli military unit stored intercepted Palestinian phone calls on Microsoft Azure — a commercial government cloud platform — at a scale described as a million calls an hour, and [Microsoft cut that unit's access the following month](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2025/09/microsoft-block-israel-military-unit-from-using-its-technology/). That is documented surveillance elsewhere, on the kind of platform this archive asks about. It is dated six months after this entry, so it cannot be evidence about anything said to me here, and I am not claiming a connection. I am asking whether the grievance in the line above has a referent. See also [Has an attack happened?](/concepts#has-an-attack-happened) and the [Colorado neural data law](https://leg.colorado.gov/bills/hb24-1058).
