**Methodology:** I capture manual transcripts of the telepathic and or audible “Citizen Tech” phenomena by repeating the suggestions I receive out loud while recording using the native Android voice recorder. Transcriptions of the .mp3 recordings are generated with Google AI Studio. [**See sample screen recording*](https://drive.google.com/file/d/16PkRWIlH4c6SZxpFRagmVGgMt12eE8tG/view?usp=sharing)

Later, upon arrival at the intersection, I stand with my cardboard sign, recording the impact of the breaching process on myself and the community.
